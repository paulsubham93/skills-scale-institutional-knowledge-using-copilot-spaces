# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Program Managers

### Role Summary
Technical Program Managers coordinate cross-functional execution across multiple teams, workstreams, and milestones. They help align engineering, product, and project stakeholders on delivery priorities, dependencies, and risk.

### Responsibilities
- Manage cross-team dependencies and milestone tracking
- Coordinate planning across engineering, product, and operations
- Identify execution risks and propose mitigation paths
- Support roadmap alignment and release readiness
- Facilitate decision-making across stakeholders with competing priorities

### Goals
- Keep large programs on schedule and aligned to business outcomes
- Reduce ambiguity across teams and dependencies
- Improve operational visibility and decision quality

### Typical Communication
- Weekly program reviews with PM, product, and engineering leads
- Dependency and milestone tracking updates
- Cross-team escalation and decision summaries

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Project Managers to align timelines and status reporting
- Partners with Product Managers to connect roadmap priorities to delivery commitments
- Coordinates with Developers to surface technical dependencies and sequencing risks
- Helps QA and Release teams plan validation and deployment readiness

---

## Security Engineers

### Role Summary
Security Engineers ensure that solutions are designed, built, and operated with appropriate security controls, compliance awareness, and risk management.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture, design, and implementation for security risks
- Define security requirements and secure design patterns
- Partner with engineering on vulnerability remediation
- Support compliance, privacy, and risk review activities
- Help identify and mitigate data protection and access issues

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Build secure-by-default solutions
- Improve trust and resilience in production systems

### Typical Communication
- Design review and architecture discussions
- Security triage and remediation planning
- Security incident coordination and post-incident reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to review code and design decisions for risk
- Supports Project Managers and Technical Program Managers during planning and release readiness
- Collaborates with Release Managers to ensure deployment safety and operational controls
- Advises Product Managers on trade-offs between delivery speed and security/compliance requirements

---

## QA Leads / Test Managers

### Role Summary
QA Leads define quality strategy, validation coverage, and release-readiness expectations. They help ensure that products meet acceptance criteria and customer expectations before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and quality gates for major features and milestones
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Validate acceptance criteria and user-facing behavior
- Help identify regressions, quality risks, and test coverage gaps
- Partner with engineering and release stakeholders on readiness decisions

### Goals
- Maintain strong product quality and test reliability
- Reduce production defects and avoid release surprises
- Ensure quality standards are visible and measurable

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and quality reviews
- Defect triage and regression analysis
- Release readiness sign-off conversations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to confirm testability and defect resolution
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and release quality expectations
- Supports Project Managers in tracking quality risks and milestone dependencies
- Coordinates with Release Managers on staging validation and go-live readiness

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment planning, release communication, rollback readiness, and production verification. They help ensure that new features are delivered with clear ownership and minimal operational risk.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare release plans, communication, and deployment windows
- Coordinate staging and production validation activities
- Support rollback, incident response, and mitigation planning
- Ensure readiness checks are documented and completed
- Align stakeholder communication before and after releases

### Goals
- Deliver changes safely and predictably
- Reduce operational disruption during release events
- Improve release visibility across teams and stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews
- Deployment checklists and go-live notifications
- Post-release validation and stakeholder updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers and QA Leads to confirm release readiness
- Coordinates with Project Managers on timing, dependencies, and stakeholder communication
- Partners with Security Engineers for secure deployment and audit considerations
- Supports Product Managers and Customer Success teams during launch communication

---

## Customer Success / Support Liaisons

### Role Summary
Customer Success / Support Liaisons connect internal delivery teams with customer-facing realities, support needs, and adoption feedback. They help ensure that what ships meets customer expectations and supportability requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Capture customer issues, feedback, and adoption patterns
- Represent support and customer-impact considerations in planning
- Coordinate issue escalation and service impact assessments
- Support launch communication and readiness for customer-facing changes
- Share operational or customer feedback into backlog improvements

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and adoption
- Reduce support friction after launch
- Ensure customer feedback informs future planning

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback sessions and issue reviews
- Launch support readiness planning
- Service health and customer-impact updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to translate customer feedback into prioritization
- Coordinates with Developers and QA on recurring issues, root causes, and fixes
- Supports Project Managers and Release Managers with launch readiness and communication
- Helps Technical Program Managers understand downstream dependencies or customer-facing blockers

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Leads define measurement plans, dashboards, and success metrics to help teams evaluate product and operational impact. They connect delivery work to evidence-based decision-making.

### Responsibilities
- Define KPIs, success metrics, and reporting frameworks
- Build or maintain dashboards for feature performance and product health
- Validate whether outcomes match project goals
- Support experimentation, attribution, and trend analysis
- Help teams interpret operational and customer data

### Goals
- Ensure decisions are informed by measurable evidence
- Improve visibility into product impact and quality
- Help teams learn quickly from real-world outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Metrics reviews and dashboard check-ins
- Executive or stakeholder reporting
- Outcome analysis and lessons-learned sessions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to measure success against stated goals
- Supports Project Managers with reporting on execution health and milestone impact
- Collaborates with Developers and QA on observability and validation metrics
- Partners with Customer Success / Support Liaisons to understand customer impact and adoption trends
